How to ride the metro in China

Cheap, clean, exact. But there is an airport-style X-ray at every station door, and the staff may ask you to sip your water bottle.

Step by step

  1. Bag on the belt, walk through the arch. If you carry a drink, sip it when they point at it. That proves it is water.
  2. In Alipay, tap Transport (出行), choose the city, and a ride QR appears. Hold it to the reader at the gate. Same code on the way out.
  3. No phone signal underground? Buy a single ticket at the machine with cash or by tapping your Alipay code on the screen.
  4. Stand right on escalators in Beijing. In Shanghai, no eating or drinking in the carriage, fines are real.

Where to go

Beijing Line 1 for Tiananmen, Line 2 around the old city. Shanghai Line 2 links both airports through People's Square and the Bund.

The one mistake

Arriving at 23:15. Most lines stop around 23:00 and taxis outside stations are the expensive kind.
