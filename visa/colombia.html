Do Colombians need a visa for China?

Checked 17 September 2026 against official Chinese sources

签

Colombia passport · ordinary

Yes. Apply for a visa before you fly.

As of 17 September 2026, Colombia is not on China's 30-day visa-free list, has no mutual exemption agreement for ordinary passports, and is not among the 57 countries eligible for 240-hour transit.

Your options

  • Tourist (L) visa. Apply at a Chinese embassy, consulate or Chinese visa application service center in your country. For single and double-entry visas of up to 180 days, fingerprints are currently waived and fees reduced until 31 December 2026.
  • 24-hour direct transit. Any nationality can transit through a Chinese airport within 24 hours without a visa, but must stay in the port area.

Lists change. Colombia could be added to a visa-free or transit list; this page is checked against official announcements and was last checked on 17 September 2026.

Questions people ask

Can Colombians use China's 240-hour visa-free transit?

No. As of 17 September 2026, Colombia is not among the 57 eligible countries.

Is there any visa-free way for Colombians to enter China?

Only 24-hour direct transit, staying in the airport or port area.

Where do Colombians apply for a China visa?

At a Chinese embassy or consulate, or a Chinese visa application service center, in the country where you live.
