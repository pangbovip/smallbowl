Do Malaysians need a visa for China?

Checked 17 September 2026 against official Chinese sources

免

Malaysia passport · ordinary

No visa needed: 30 days per stay, 90 in any 180.

China and Malaysia have a mutual visa exemption agreement for ordinary passports in force since 17 July 2025. Unlike China's one-sided visa-free list, an agreement has no end date, though either side can suspend it.

What to have ready at the border

  • An ordinary passport valid for your whole stay. Your airline may apply stricter validity checks.
  • A return or onward ticket and your hotel bookings. You may be asked about your plans.
  • The arrival card. Since November 2025 you can fill it in online before you land.

What it does not cover

  • Mutual exemption agreements are for short visits. Work, study and journalism need a visa.
  • If you plan to stay longer than the limit above, apply for a visa before you travel.

Questions people ask

How long can Malaysians stay in China without a visa?

30 days per stay, 90 in any 180, under the mutual visa exemption agreement between China and Malaysia.

Does the agreement expire?

Mutual agreements have no end date, unlike China's one-sided visa-free list. Either country can suspend one, so check before long trips.

Can I work in China under the agreement?

No. It covers short visits such as tourism, business and family visits. Work, study and journalism need a visa.
